💡 Key Costing Notes:

  • Apple does NOT charge you $99 twice: Apple Support upgrades your existing Personal account to an Organization account for FREE once your DUNS number is ready.
  • Google charges $25 for each account: Google does not have an in-place conversion button, so you open a business account for $25 and use Google's free "Transfer App" tool.

3. The Big Trap with a Google Play Personal Account ⚠️

Google's 14-Day 12-Tester Restriction (Personal Accounts Only)

Many developers create a Personal Google Play account thinking it is faster because it does not need a DUNS number. However, Google introduced a strict policy:

  • Personal accounts CANNOT publish directly to the public.
  • You must recruit at least 12 people with Android phones to install your app in Closed Testing and keep it installed for 14 continuous days.
  • Only after 14 days can you apply for public release.
  • On a Business account, this 14-day rule DOES NOT exist! You can release directly to production immediately.

4. Converting from Personal to Business: What Happens? (100% Safe)

Is it Safe to Start with Personal and Convert to Business Later? YES!

Both Google and Apple provide official, sanctioned tools to transition smoothly. Here is what happens:

  • Existing Users: Users do NOT have to delete or reinstall anything. The app remains on their phones.
  • User Data & Bookings: 100% Safe. Your database and backend are completely separate and untouched. Customer logins, bookings, and profiles remain intact.
  • Reviews & 5-Star Ratings: 100% Preserved. Every single review, star rating, and download count transfers over.
  • Store URLs: The download links stay identical.
  • What actually changes: Only the developer name shown on the store updates from your personal name to your official company name.

5. Is the DUNS Number Still Valid for That Account?

YES, 100% VALID!

  • A D-U-N-S Number is issued by Dun & Bradstreet to your legal business entity, not to any personal Google or Apple account.
  • It is permanent and tied to your company name and address.
  • When your DUNS number arrives, Google and Apple will accept it immediately to verify your business. They will never reject it because you had a personal account before.
